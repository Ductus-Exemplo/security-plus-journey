# CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 — Study Resources

## Introduction

I passed the CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 certification in November 2026.

This page contains the main learning resources I used during my preparation.

My approach was straightforward: **study one domain, attempt questions on that domain, review topics I struggled with, and then move to the next domain.**

After completing all five domains, I moved to mixed practice questions covering the entire syllabus.

Most of my learning resources were freely available on YouTube.

### How I Used the Questions

My process was:

1. Finish the lesson for a particular domain.
2. Attempt questions focused on that domain.
3. Identify questions I answered incorrectly or found difficult.
4. Review the relevant topic until I understood the concept.
5. Continue to the next domain.

I found this approach helpful because it allowed me to identify knowledge gaps early.

## 4. Moving to Mixed Practice Questions

Once I had completed all five domains, I moved to mixed questions covering the full Security+ syllabus.

This helped me practice identifying which security concept applied without already knowing the domain.

When reviewing incorrect answers, I tried to understand:

- Why was the correct answer the best choice?
- Why were the other options less appropriate?
- Which concept did I misunderstand?
- What should I revisit before attempting similar questions?

## 5. My Advice to Other Learners

My biggest recommendation is to avoid treating practice questions as a memorization exercise.

Try to understand the concept behind every question, especially when two answer choices appear similar.

You also don't necessarily need multiple full courses. A main learning resource, focused practice, and targeted review can provide a useful preparation structure.

Everyone learns differently, so adapt this approach to your own experience and schedule.
