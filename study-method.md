# How I Studied for CompTIA Security+ SY0-701

**My result: Passed with a score of 797 — November 2026**

## Introduction

I created this page to share how I prepared for the CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 certification.

My study approach was based on understanding one domain at a time, practicing questions on that domain, identifying areas I struggled with, and revisiting those concepts before moving forward.

I primarily used free YouTube resources, focused practice questions, and review sessions.

## Step 1 — Follow a Main Video Course

I started with a primary Security+ video course and worked through the five exam domains in order.

**My main learning resource:**

[Security+ SY0-701 Exam Cram Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL7XJSuT7Dq_UDJgYoQGIW9viwM5hc4C7n)

Rather than jumping between different study materials, I used this as my main source for understanding the syllabus.

## Step 2 — Practice Questions After Each Domain

After studying a domain, I moved to questions focused specifically on that domain.

My process was:

1. Complete the domain walkthrough.
2. Attempt domain-specific practice questions.
3. Identify questions I answered incorrectly or found confusing.
4. Review the underlying concepts.
5. Continue to the next domain.

I repeated this approach from Domain 1 through Domain 5.

## Step 3 — Revisit Difficult Concepts

I used Professor Messer's Security+ videos as an additional review resource whenever I needed a clearer explanation of a concept.

[Professor Messer's Security+ SY0-701 Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLG49S3nxzAnl4QDVqK-hOnoqcSKEIDDuv)

Some concepts I spent additional time understanding included:

- Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), certificate authorities, and trust chains
- Symmetric and asymmetric cryptography
- Risk calculations and terminology
- RTO and RPO
- Authentication protocols, including EAP and RADIUS
- Network segmentation and firewall rule ordering

I focused on understanding why an answer was correct, rather than only memorizing the answer.

## Step 4 — Move to Mixed Practice Questions

After completing all five domains, I moved to mixed questions covering the full Security+ syllabus.

This helped me practice identifying the relevant cybersecurity concept without knowing which domain a question came from.

I also practiced explaining why the incorrect answer options were unsuitable.

## Step 5 — Review Mistakes and Improve

Whenever I struggled with a topic, I returned to the relevant study material and worked through additional examples.

This helped me identify knowledge gaps and improve my confidence with scenario-based questions.

## My Key Takeaways

- Studying one domain at a time helped me organize the material.
- Practicing questions revealed concepts I needed to revisit.
- Reviewing incorrect answers helped improve my understanding.
- Mixed practice questions helped prepare me for scenario-based reasoning.
- Understanding the concepts was more useful than memorizing answer letters.
