**My Cybersecurity Learning Journey**

**About This Repository**

Welcome to my cybersecurity learning portfolio!

I recently passed the **CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 certification**.

I created this repository to document my cybersecurity learning journey, share useful study resources, and demonstrate the practical skills I continue to develop.

## Why I Started This Project

While preparing for Security+, I discovered that understanding cybersecurity concepts is more valuable than simply memorizing definitions.

Some topics I initially found challenging included:

- Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) and certificate trust chains
- Risk calculations and risk management terminology
- Authentication protocols such as EAP and RADIUS
- Network segmentation and firewall rules
- Business continuity and recovery strategies

Through practice questions, hands-on examples, and continued revision, I developed a stronger understanding of these topics.
